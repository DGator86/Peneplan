## Engineer View Addition

In Engineer View, an additional block:

```
CRYSTALLINE SYSTEM CONTINUITY — ELEVATOR PIT

Penetron Admixture: integral to all pit concrete elements (floor + walls).
Crystalline matrix forms within the concrete capillary structure.

PENECRETE MORTAR at:
  • Floor-to-wall construction joint (keyway treatment before wall pour)
  • All conduit sleeves (collar applied before and after pour)
  • Sump/drain sleeve (collar + hydraulic cement at drain body)

Waterproofing continuity is maintained through the concrete substrate itself —
not through a discrete applied membrane layer.

No applied membrane = no membrane termination, no lap seams, no corner details
requiring independent maintenance or inspection.

⚠ Note: Expansion joints and designed movement joints require separate mechanical
treatment. This pathway applies to construction joints only.
```

## "Why This Works" Expandable

Below the comparison table, a collapsible explanation:

```
[ Why does Penetron eliminate so many failure points in a pit? ▼ ]
```

On expand:
```
Traditional pit waterproofing fails at the same places on every project:

Inside corners — sheet membrane cannot conform to a tight 90° angle.
Penetration boots — field-cut openings around conduits and drains
  are inconsistent and prone to adhesion failure.
Termination bars — the membrane edge at the top of the pit is a
  constant maintenance point.
Confined space — installers working in a 10×10 pit at the bottom of
  an excavation cannot achieve the quality control they can on open walls.

Penetron eliminates these failure modes by making the waterproofing
integral to the concrete itself. There is no membrane to terminate, lap,
boot, or corner-flash. The concrete IS the waterproofing.
```
