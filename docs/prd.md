# PenePlan — Product Requirements Document
## Phase 1: Product Definition

---

## 1. Product Name & Tagline

**Product Name:** PenePlan

**Tagline:** Reduce Time. Simplify Construction. Stay Dry.

Each phrase maps to a direct GC pain point:
- **Reduce Time** → schedule compression
- **Simplify Construction** → fewer phases, trades, and interfaces
- **Stay Dry** → ultimate project outcome

---

## 2. The Promise

> Identify hidden cost, sequencing complexity, and leak-path exposure in below-grade construction — and show the quantified impact of switching to integrated Penetron waterproofing.

PenePlan is positioned as a **below-grade construction simplification platform**, not waterproofing sales software. This framing is intentional: it gives the platform credibility with engineers, consultants, and sophisticated GCs who would reject an overtly promotional tool.

---

## 3. Primary Project Category

**Underground membrane replacement for structural foundations.**

This covers:
- Below-grade foundation walls (cast-in-place concrete)
- Below-grade slabs on grade and suspended slabs
- Elevator pits (special module — monolithic pathway)
- Retaining walls and planter walls with waterproofing
- Parking structure below-grade decks
- Utility tunnels and vaults

The platform evaluates replacing traditional applied-membrane systems (sheet, fluid-applied, bentonite) with Penetron crystalline systems integrated into the concrete itself.

---

## 4. Core Outputs

The platform produces six primary outputs for every analysis:

| Output | Description |
|---|---|
| **Savings Range** | Estimated cost differential between traditional membrane scope and Penetron conversion (low/mid/high range) |
| **Schedule Savings** | Estimated days/phases removed by eliminating membrane installation, protection board, drainage board, and associated sequencing holds |
| **Failure-Point Reduction** | Count of discrete waterproofing failure points eliminated (terminations, laps, transitions, penetrations) |
| **Sequencing Simplification** | Trades, mobilizations, and critical-path constraints removed |
| **Penetron Conversion Recommendation** | System recommendation (admixture, crystalline coating, joint treatment, or combination) with rationale |
| **PDF Report** | Branded, shareable document summarizing all outputs for use in VE proposals, owner conversations, and engineer/consultant review |

---

## 5. What PenePlan Does NOT Do

These boundaries must be communicated clearly at every touchpoint to maintain credibility and manage liability:

- **No engineering approval.** PenePlan does not approve, certify, or stamp any waterproofing design. All outputs are estimating and constructability evaluation tools only.
- **No warranty approval.** System warranty eligibility requires Penetron technical review; PenePlan outputs do not constitute warranty commitment.
- **No structural redesign.** The platform does not evaluate structural capacity, drainage design, or hydrostatic pressure calculations.
- **No specification authority.** Outputs are not contract documents. Conversion requires spec modification through the engineer of record.
- **No site-specific conditions analysis.** Soil conditions, water table, ground movement, and site hydrology require field investigation outside the scope of this tool.

These limitations appear in:
- A persistent disclaimer on the results page
- The PDF report footer and disclaimer section
- A dedicated disclaimer page in the PDF

## 10. Elevator Pit Special Module

Elevator pits represent a high-value, high-confidence conversion pathway:

- Traditional approach: pre-applied membrane, protection board, poured pit walls, membrane laps at all transitions, penetration seals at conduits/sump, termination bar at top
- Penetron monolithic approach: admixture in pour, crystalline coating at transitions, joint treatment at construction joints — single trade, single pour event, zero sheet membrane
- Failure-point reduction in this zone is typically dramatic (6–12+ failure points eliminated)
- This module activates automatically when the intake form includes "elevator pit" in scope

The elevator pit output is designed to be the strongest single "holy shit" moment in the report.

## 13. Strategic Platform Position

PenePlan functions as a **conversion and validation platform** for the entire below-grade decision chain. It is not a niche estimator.

It creates a **common language** between stakeholders who currently operate in silos:
- GC uses cost/schedule language
- Engineer uses technical/constructability language
- Sub uses field execution language
- Owner uses lifecycle/risk language
- Supplier uses scope/yardage language

PenePlan translates the same underlying analysis into each stakeholder's native framing. That's the competitive moat.
